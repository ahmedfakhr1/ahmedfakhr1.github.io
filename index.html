Mosaïque

Rooftop restaurant, Korba, Heliopolis

A phone-first website in English and Arabic, with a live menu of about 140 dishes that the staff update from a Google Sheet.

Before this, the menu was a 53-page PDF linked from Instagram. The new menu page scores 97 on Google PageSpeed for mobile and opens with no signal after the first visit.

LÜLU’S

Restaurant, New Cairo

A quiet landing page and a menu you leaf through like a book, page by page, set exactly as the restaurant's own printed menu.

The menu opens on morning or evening by the time in Cairo. Colours and photos change with the season, and the team edits every word and price from a Google Sheet.

What comes with every site

A look that is already yours
Your logo, colours, photos and menu set the design. Nothing starts from a template.
A menu your staff can edit
Dishes and prices live in a Google Sheet. Change a cell and the site follows.
Built for the phone
Most guests arrive on a phone, often from a QR code on the table, so the phone layout is designed first and kept light.
Everything in your name
The domain, the hosting and the files sit in your own accounts, and I hand them over with a guide for your team.
