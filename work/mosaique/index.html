Mosaïque: a phone-first website and live menu for a Korba rooftop restaurant

Mosaïque had a strong brand, great photos and 19K Instagram followers, but no website. Guests reached the menu through a PDF link in the Instagram bio. I built a bilingual site that opens in about a second on a phone, a menu the staff update from a Google Sheet, and a table QR code that lands straight on it.

Client Mosaïque, Korba, HeliopolisRole design, build, hosting setupLanguages English and ArabicHosting cost free, domain only

Before and after

Almost every visitor arrives on a phone, many from a QR code on the table. The old route asked them to download a long PDF over mobile data.

Before

Instagram and a PDF

  • No website. Google Maps listed no site.
  • Menu: a 53-page PDF of about 8 MB on Google Drive, linked from the Instagram bio.
  • Every price change meant a new PDF and a new link.
  • Booking only by phone call.
  • Nothing for Google to find when people searched for the menu.
After

A site built for the phone

  • Home, menu and visit pages in English and Arabic, matched to the brand book.
  • Searchable menu of about 140 dishes in 19 sections, with photos and a sticky section bar.
  • Staff edit prices and dishes in a Google Sheet; the site updates on its own.
  • Three-step table request that sends a ready WhatsApp message.
  • Table QR code with the logo in the middle, printable from the menu page.

How it works

  • The sheet is the back office. Menu, sections, site texts and phone numbers live in one Google Sheet with dropdowns and checks. A scheduled job copies it into the site every 30 minutes.
  • Mistakes don't reach guests. If a price isn't a number or half the menu disappears at once, nothing is published and the site keeps its last good menu.
  • One place for every setting. The address, contact details and credit live in one config file; a build step writes them into every page, the sitemap and the QR code.
  • Built for weak signal. No framework, photos in three sizes in AVIF and WebP, long caching, and an offline copy of the menu after the first visit.
  • Bookings that arrive complete. The request form checks names and Egyptian or international phone numbers, accepts Arabic digits, and sends one clean WhatsApp message.
